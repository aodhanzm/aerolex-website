Why the current mark clashes

The existing mark uses a saturated cool blue gradient with glossy 3D highlights and a heavy geometric sans wordmark — three SaaS-app conventions. The site is the opposite: warm off-white, flat hairline rules, editorial serif, monospace system labels. To cohere, the mark needs to lose the gradient, lose the gloss, and the wordmark needs to come from the same type family the rest of the site already uses (Newsreader or Geist Mono).
