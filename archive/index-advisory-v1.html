§ 03 — Product suite

Four waves, built to compound. Each one ships standalone.

Wave 01 · in build

Command Center →

Real-time engagement visibility with assignment dispatch. Partners see every active engagement on one screen — overdue, stalled, on-track — and dispatch work straight from it. Consultants acknowledge in one tap.

engagement tracking dispatch + ack workload view
Wave 02 · roadmap

Integrations & Email Agent

Meet teams where they already work. Slack and Teams notifications route Command Center assignments into your existing chat. The email agent reads inbound mail, classifies by engagement, and extracts action items for review.

slack / teams email triage action extraction
Wave 03 · roadmap

Meeting Pipeline

Meeting-to-execution pipeline. Transcript in, structured minutes and assigned action items out. Dependency chains flag which items are blocked, by what, and surface them when the blocker clears.

transcript ingest action dispatch dependency graph
Wave 04 · roadmap

Intelligence & Contract Review

AI contract review with extracted dates, values, and flagged clauses. Cross-engagement pattern analysis. Knowledge search across firm data — "what did we tell this client about pricing two years ago?"

contract extraction knowledge search redline review
§ 04 — Consulting

Process audits and custom builds for workflows beyond the standard waves.

AeroLex runs a consulting practice alongside the product suite, for firms whose workflow pain doesn't map cleanly to Command Center, Email Agent, Meeting Pipeline, or Contract Review. Every engagement begins by documenting how the operation actually runs at the level a partner doesn't see day to day. Then identifying the single change that resolves the actual constraint without disturbing what already functions.

Project-based, scoped to one workflow per engagement. Capped at two concurrent engagements.

Engagement note

Most consulting engagements run four to ten weeks and ship a single system the team uses on Monday. If a custom build generalizes into a product feature, it joins the roadmap.
