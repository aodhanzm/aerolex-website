THE SCENE working title

Learn Spanish by living it.

You arrive in a city where you do not speak the language, and you have a night to get through. There are people at the bar who will talk to you. What you can say to them is the whole game. The words you pick up stay picked up, because you needed them for something.

No lessons, no streak to protect, nobody grading you. Just a place, some people, and the very real feeling of not having the word you need yet.

Play in your browser Free while it is in testing. Works best on a laptop or desktop. Making an account needs an invite code. You can also play without one and skip the account entirely.
What a night looks like

You get ready

The evening starts in your flat. You decide what you are going to try to say tonight, and then you go out, which is the part that is actually difficult.

You talk to people

Conversations are built out of what you can say, not out of a menu of correct answers. Say something once and it is gone; the people you meet remember what you told them.

You take words home

Words you actually used are the ones you keep. They come back to you later, in someone else's sentence, on a night when you need them.
