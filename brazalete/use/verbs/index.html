What do the controls mean?

MODO chooses the mood: indicativo, subjuntivo, or imperativo. TIEMPO chooses a tense or non-finite form. PERSONA chooses who the verb refers to. Until you choose one, the centre shows the infinitive; tap the chosen person again to go back to it.

GÉNERO and NÚMERO set adjective agreement. SÍ / NO adds or removes negation for verbs.

Gray, unlit choices are unavailable for the current combination. A gerund or participle has no grammatical person. A participle on its own is not a complete past-tense sentence.

For Vos, the main subjunctive uses forms such as comas. Forms such as comás also occur regionally. The pronoun remains te, never os.

For llamarse and levantarse, the verb display includes the required reflexive pronoun except on the bare participle. The separate disc lets you explore pronouns; confirming it does not automatically attach another one.

Keyboard: Tab reaches every labelled control. With the device focused, the Up and Down arrow keys choose a dial, the Left and Right arrow keys change it, Enter confirms, Esc clears the person (back to the infinitive), N changes SÍ / NO, and T presses the Tú/Vos switch on the verb device. On the pronoun disc, the Left and Right arrow keys select a cell, V changes its role, and Enter confirms. Arrow shortcuts stay inside the focused device.
