The world of The Scene

Solmara, an island where you arrive without a word of Spanish

The Scene is a Spanish-learning game set on Solmara, a Y2K-futuristic island city.

Arriving on Solmara

You land on Solmara without a word of Spanish, and you have to talk your way through the night. Nobody there speaks your language yet. Two twin guides, Carmen and Cayetana, help you find the words.

The reasons I built a world at all, and the games that inspired it, are in the design story.

What you wear

The grammar lives on gadgets the player wears, because a person mid-conversation won’t dig out a notebook. The Brazalete is the one for verbs. A pronoun disc handles the words that sit before them. The game has more gadgets, and I’ll describe each one here when it’s ready to show.

The Brazalete design story covers how the first one was built.

Who made it

I led a team of ten at a language tutoring company to build The Scene: five language tutors, three engineers and two UI and UX designers. We ran a playtest with 100 students. The full game is being built now, and I still oversee production.

Try it

The Brazalete is open to everyone and works on its own, so you can try the gadget the game is built around. The full game is in a private friends beta.
